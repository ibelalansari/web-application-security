OWASP TOP 10 — A06: INSECURE DESIGN
====================================

Purpose
-------
Insecure Design represents weaknesses caused by missing or ineffective security
controls at the design and architectural level.

Unlike implementation bugs, insecure design often exists because the required
security control was never properly designed.

Common Examples
---------------
- Missing abuse-case analysis
- Missing rate limiting
- Weak business logic
- Inadequate authorization model
- Unsafe workflow design
- Missing transaction integrity controls
- Lack of segregation of duties
- Security assumptions not enforced

Testing Methodology
-------------------
- Understand the application's intended workflow.
- Identify security assumptions.
- Identify trust boundaries.
- Identify sensitive business operations.
- Test abnormal workflow sequences.
- Test repeated operations.
- Test race-prone business processes.
- Test limits and business rules.
- Attempt actions outside the intended workflow.
- Compare expected behavior with actual behavior.

Business Logic Testing
----------------------
Focus on:
- Quantity manipulation
- Price manipulation
- Workflow skipping
- Repeated actions
- State transition abuse
- Role restrictions
- Transaction manipulation
- Approval bypass
- Account recovery workflows

Impact
------
- Financial loss
- Unauthorized transactions
- Privilege escalation
- Fraud
- Business process abuse
- Data compromise

Remediation
-----------
- Perform threat modeling.
- Define security requirements before implementation.
- Model abuse cases.
- Implement business rules server-side.
- Apply defense in depth.
- Use centralized security controls.
- Test security assumptions during design and development.

Security Principle
------------------
Security should be designed into the application's architecture rather than
added after implementation.
